## 🌸 About me

I'm a **Cybersecurity Analyst working in a Security Operations Center (SOC)**, with hands-on experience in **incident investigation, response and threat hunting**.

I enjoy connecting evidence from different sources to understand what happened, determine the scope of an incident and support an effective response. I also work on improving detection logic and response procedures based on what investigations reveal.

- 🔎 **Day to day:** investigating alerts and correlating SIEM, EDR, identity, email and network evidence.
- 🛡️ **Security operations:** incident containment, threat hunting, detection tuning and response playbooks.
- 🐍 **Building:** Python tools that support security analysis and make investigation results easier to review.
- 🎓 **Education:** completing a Cyber Defense degree at FIAP — expected graduation in December 2026.
- 🌱 **Exploring:** security automation, digital forensics and the intersection of AI and cybersecurity.
- 📍 **Based in:** São Paulo, Brazil.
